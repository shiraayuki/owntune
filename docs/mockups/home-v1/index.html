Accent

Home becomes two tabs

  • For You is the quick start: what you played last, the queue to continue, shuffle buttons and rows built from play counts, last played and date added.
  • Stats shows how you listen: time per day, streak, top artists, songs and albums for a period, time of day, genres, a monthly recap and a library overview.
  • Sub-tabs use the underlined text tabs chosen for Library. The period switch inside Stats is a row of small chips, so it cannot be mistaken for another tab level. The title row shows the greeting by time of day on both tabs.

1 · For You

Six tiles of what you played last (playlists, albums, Liked Songs), the queue to continue, two shuffle buttons, then rows.

2 · Stats

Built from data the app keeps (plays with time stamps, genres from tags, file sizes). The chips switch the period for everything on the tab except the library overview and the all-time list.
