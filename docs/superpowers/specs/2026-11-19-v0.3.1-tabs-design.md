# v0.3.1 — Four tabs with a Library tab (design)

**Size:** small change. **Mockups:** `docs/mockups/m2-v1/` screens 01–06 and the "Library" back label in 07. The player parts of those mockups (mini player, full-screen player, Up Next, "Play Next/Later", active Play/Shuffle) are **not** part of v0.3.1; they belong to M2 (v0.4.0).

## Intent

Five tabs felt like too many. The tab bar goes back to four items, and Songs, Albums and Artists move together under one Library tab. This replaces the "Tab bar has five items" point of the v0.3.0 addendum (`2026-11-19-m1-v0.3.0-design.md`).

## What gets built

- **Tab bar, four items, in this order:** Home (`CupertinoIcons.house` / `house_fill`), Search (`search`), Library (`square_stack` / `square_stack_fill`), Playlists (`music_note_list`). Items share the width evenly, as now.
- **Home:** placeholder. The title row reads "Home", there is no search field, and the empty state shows the house glyph with "Nothing here yet" / "Home arrives in a later version." The "…" menu has only Settings. What goes on Home is decided later, once the rest of the app is done.
- **Library:** the title row reads "Library". Under it sits a segmented control with Songs | Albums | Artists, styled like `.segmented` in the mockup CSS (the one in Settings). Under that, each sub-tab shows its own screen unchanged (search field, Songs meta line, sections and A–Z index, album grid, artist list). Each sub-tab keeps its own query and scroll position when you switch between sub-tabs or tabs. The "…" menu shows the sort options only while the Songs sub-tab is active, and Settings always.
- **Search and Playlists:** unchanged, only their position in the tab bar changes.
- **Back labels:** screens opened from the Library sub-tabs, or from the song menu there, show "Library" as their back label (it was "Songs", "Albums" or "Artists"). Back labels from detail screens (album title, artist name) and from Search stay as they are.
- **"+" menu:** unchanged, top right on every tab.
- **Version:** `0.3.1`; update CLAUDE.md (status, tab list) and add an addendum note to the v0.3.0 spec pointing here.

## Approaches

1. **Library as one tab that holds the three existing screens in an `IndexedStack`, switched by a segmented control** (chosen). The screens stay as they are, and their state (queries, scroll) survives switching because they stay mounted. Changes stay in the shell plus a small `LibraryTab`.
2. A real `TabBarView` with swipe between sub-tabs. Swiping fights the A–Z index drag and the horizontal swipe on detail screens, and it is not in the mockup.
3. Sub-tabs as pushed screens from a Library list (as in Apple Music). That means an extra tap to reach Songs, which goes against the goal of fewer, flatter tabs.

## My calls (please check)

- **The app opens on the Library tab (Songs) while Home is a placeholder**, so launching the app does not land on an empty screen. Once Home has content it becomes the start tab.
- The selected sub-tab is kept in memory only, not stored in the settings; after a restart it is Songs again.
- The segmented control from the Settings sheet moves into a shared widget, so both places use the same one.
