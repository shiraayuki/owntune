# v0.8.2: auto-advance fix, swipe in Now Playing, heart in the mini player

Approved 2026-11-25. A small player release before the Distribution milestone.

## Why

- **Bug (iOS):** when a song ends, the next one (Songs list, Up Next, playlist) does not start; the user has to press Next. just_audio keeps `playing` true after the end, so the controller's `play()` after loading the next file was a no-op and playback relied on the native player resuming by itself, which iOS did not do right after the end (AVQueuePlayer pauses itself at the item end). Skip later works because that moment has passed.
- **Wishes:** skip by swiping in the full-screen player (like Spotify), and like the current song from the mini player without opening the full player.

## Decided

1. **Fix:** on completion the controller sets the state to paused and pauses the engine, then loads the next entry and starts it with a real `play()` (just_audio's own advice). A newer load during that pause wins (generation check). End of queue with repeat off behaves as before. Verified by a unit test; final proof only on the iPhone.
2. **Swipe on the Now Playing cover** (only the big cover; the Up Next view has none):
   - The cover follows the finger; the neighbour's cover (next to the left side, previous to the right) slides in beside it, like Spotify.
   - Skip at a quarter of the width or a fast fling (same thresholds as the mini player); else it springs back.
   - Left = Next; where there is no next song the drag resists (same factor as the mini player).
   - Right = `skipBack()`: always the previous song regardless of the position, on the first song a restart (as the mini player). The Previous button keeps restart-after-3-s.
   - Neighbours come from the queue: next = `nextIndex(auto: false)` entry (wraps under repeat all), previous = `previousIndex` entry; with none, no neighbour cover is shown.
3. **Heart in the mini player**, left of play/pause: outline heart, or filled accent heart when liked; tap toggles the like (`setLiked`) and does not open the player; a failure shows `showLikeError`, like the Now Playing heart. Tooltip "Add to Liked Songs" / "Remove from Liked Songs".

## Non-goals

No vertical gestures, no swipe on title/controls, no change to the Previous button, no haptics.

## Left to the builder

Animation details (spring-back duration, how the committed skip animates out); the mini player's swipe area keeps excluding the buttons.
