What changes

  • Four tabs instead of five: Home (placeholder for now), Search, Library, Playlists.
  • Library holds Songs, Albums and Artists as sub-tabs (segmented control under the title). Each sub-tab keeps its own search field and query; the sort options in "…" only show on Songs.
  • Mini player floats above the tab bar on every tab and also on pushed screens (album, artist). Tap opens the player; pause and next work in place; a thin line shows progress.
  • Player slides up as a full-screen sheet and closes with a swipe down. Background is a blurred copy of the cover. Shuffle and repeat flank the main controls (repeat cycles off → all → one).
  • Up Next replaces the cover inside the player: reorder by dragging, swipe to remove, "Clear".
  • Song menu gains "Play Next" and "Play Later". Play/Shuffle on album and artist pages become active.

Lock screen and Control Center (title, artist, cover, play/pause, skip, scrubbing) are system UI and not mocked. Not in these mockups: volume slider, AirPlay/output picker.
