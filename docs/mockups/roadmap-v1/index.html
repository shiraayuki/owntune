Order of the remaining work

  1. v0.5.1 – rest of M3: covers (choose from Photos or Files, crop square, remove; written into the file) and multi-select in Songs (Edit Info, Delete; Add to Playlist once M4 exists).
  2. Downloader (new milestone, before M4): "Search Online" in the "+" menu opens a YouTube-style search; tap a result, confirm, and the song lands in the library in its original format (M4A from YouTube, MP3/AAC from SoundCloud) with title, artist and the thumbnail as cover. First YouTube, then SoundCloud with the source switch.
  3. M4 – Playlists and Home: playlists with their own cover, drag & drop order, "Add to Playlist" in the song menu and multi-select, "Save to Playlist…" in the downloader. Home replaces its placeholder (recently played tiles, Recently Added, On Repeat, Rediscover, Shuffle All) and becomes the start tab again.
  4. M5 – Polish: "Open with …" / share into owntune (system UI, not mocked), Now Playing tinted by the cover, statistics in Settings.

Custom albums (formerly M4) are dropped: since v0.5.0 an album is made by giving songs the same album tag in Edit Info.

1 · v0.5.1 · Covers and multi-select

Edit Info gains a cover block. Multi-select starts from "Select" in the Library "…" menu.

2 · Downloader

"Search Online" opens on the last used source. Results are compact (smaller than YouTube itself). Songs already downloaded show "In Library".

3 · M4 · Playlists

The Playlists tab replaces its placeholder. "New Playlist" is also in the "+" menu.

4 · M4 · Home

Built from data the app already keeps (play counts, last played, date added). Becomes the start tab.

5 · M5 · Polish

"Open with …" and the share sheet are system UI; a shared file goes through the normal import sheets.
