Accent

What changes

Today both switches are the iOS segmented control: a gray track with a raised white segment. These are three more modern ways to switch the same views; everything else on the screens stays as it is.

Recommendation: A · chips. It is what Spotify does in its library and search, takes the least height, keeps every option visible with one tap, and grows naturally (SoundCloud in v0.6.1, later e.g. Playlists).

A · Filter chips

Compact pills; the selected one is filled with the accent (black in Paper). Left-aligned, scrollable if more are added. In Search Online they sit below the field, like Spotify's search filters.

B · Text tabs

Bold labels with an accent underline on the selected one, over a hairline (YouTube Music, Apple Music artist pages). Reads like real tabs; a swipe between them would fit.

C · The title is the switch

No extra row at all: the large title names the view and opens a pull-down (shown open). In Search Online the source is a small pill inside the field. Most space, but the other views are hidden behind a tap.
