Your music. Your files. Your player.

A free music player for iPhone and Android that plays the songs you own. No account, no subscription, no streaming.

Install on iPhone Download for Android

Start where you left off

Home shows what you played lately, picks up the queue you paused and keeps rows for On Repeat, Recently Added, Rediscover and the songs you never played.

A library that sorts itself

Import MP3, M4A, FLAC and WAV files from the Files app, many at once. Songs, albums and artists are grouped from the song details, with covers, and duplicates are skipped.

Keeps playing with the screen locked

A full-screen player, a mini player on every screen, Up Next, repeat and a shuffle that spreads out the same artist. Swipe the cover to skip. The lock screen and Control Center show the song too.

Playlists with your own cover

Like a song with the heart, collect songs in playlists, give them a cover and drag them into the order you want.

Find a song you don't have yet

Search YouTube Music, YouTube or SoundCloud, or paste a link, and download the song into your library with title, artist and cover. Long DJ mixes too.

Fix what the files got wrong

Change titles, artists, albums and covers, for one song or a whole album at once. The changes are written back into the files.

See what you actually listen to

Listening time per week, month or year, your streak, top artists, songs and albums, your favourite hour and your genre mix. All of it stays on your phone.

No account. No subscription. No streaming. No tracking. Just your own music, on your phone.

owntune is for people who still keep their own music files: MP3s from a CD, a Bandcamp purchase, an old iTunes library. Put them in once, and from then on everything works offline, in airplane mode and in the subway.

Install owntune

owntune is a hobby project and is not in the App Store or on Google Play. It's free, and the setup below explains every step.

  • An iPhone with iOS 15 or newer
  • A computer and a cable, only for the first setup
  • A free Apple ID
  • About 20 minutes, once
1

Install SideStore

Once, with a computer

  1. Open the official guide at docs.sidestore.io. It covers Windows, Mac and Linux.
  2. Follow it until SideStore is on your iPhone and you are signed in with your Apple ID.
  3. When it asks, turn on Developer Mode in Settings, Privacy & Security, and restart the phone.
This is the longest part. Follow the SideStore guide exactly; you only do it once.
2

Add owntune to SideStore

On the iPhone

  1. Copy this address:
    https://github.com/shiraayuki/owntune/releases/latest/download/source.json
  2. Open SideStore, go to Sources, tap ＋, paste the address and tap Add.
  3. Tap owntune in the new source, then the install button.
3

Put your music in

Any time

  1. Copy your music files into the Files app, for example iCloud Drive or On My iPhone.
  2. In owntune, tap ＋ and choose Import Music. Pick songs or a whole folder.
  3. Or tap ＋, Search Online, and download a song, or paste a link.
4

Refresh it once a week

Every 7 days

  1. Apps installed with a free Apple ID stop opening after 7 days. Your music and playlists stay.
  2. Open SideStore and tap Refresh All in My Apps, or turn on Background Refresh.

Start listening

That's it. Open owntune, pick a song and press play.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. owntune and SideStore are both free, and a free Apple ID is enough.

Where is my music stored?

Inside owntune, on your phone. The app keeps its own copy of every imported file, so deleting the original in the Files app doesn't remove it from owntune.

What happens when the 7 days run out?

owntune won't open until you refresh it in SideStore. Nothing is deleted, and after the refresh everything is back as before.

Does owntune send anything about me anywhere?

No. There is no account and no tracking. The internet is only used when you search for a song to download.

Why isn't it in the App Store?

It's a hobby project, and the song downloader would not pass the store review. Installing it yourself keeps it free.
