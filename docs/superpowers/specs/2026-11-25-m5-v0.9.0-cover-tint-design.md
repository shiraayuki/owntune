# v0.9.0 — Now Playing tinted by the cover (M5, part 1)

Decided 2026-11-25 with the user. Mockup: `docs/mockups/roadmap-v1/19-player-tinted.html`.

## Why

The full-screen player should feel like the song that is playing, like Spotify and Apple Music: every cover gives the player its own colours. Today it has a blurred cover backdrop under a dark veil, but its play button, heart and active toggles are always the user's accent.

## Decided (user)

- **Variant A (mockup 19):** background **and** controls take their colours from the cover. The play button (and the liked heart) use a light colour from the cover instead of the accent; the accent stays everywhere else in the app.
- **Way 1:** colours are computed when a song plays, from a small (64×64) version of the cover, in the background and cached per cover file; the next queue entry's cover is computed ahead. No database change.

## Decided (Claude's calls, stated to the user)

- Only the full-screen player (`player_sheet.dart`) is tinted; the mini player keeps the accent.
- Light mode ("Paper") stays monochrome and untinted.
- No cover, an unreadable cover, or colours not computed yet: the player looks as today (accent).
- Contrast is guaranteed: the background colour is darkened until white text on it reaches at least 4.5:1; the button colour is lightened until its dark icon reaches at least 4.5:1.

## Design

- **Pure colour function** (`lib/player/cover_colors.dart`): from RGBA pixels of the 64×64 image, pick the dominant colour that is vivid enough (pixels grouped into coarse colour buckets; buckets weighted by count and saturation; near-grey covers give a neutral grey). Returns `CoverColors(background, button, onButton)`:
  - `background`: that hue, darkened until white text has ≥ 4.5:1 contrast;
  - `button`: same hue, saturation capped, lightened until `onButton` (a near-black) has ≥ 4.5:1;
  - `onButton`: the near-black icon colour.
- **Provider:** a family keyed by the cover file name; decoding and resizing run in an isolate with the existing `image` package; results stay cached for the session. The player sheet also reads the provider for the next queue entry's cover, so a song change is ready.
- **Player sheet:** the blurred cover backdrop stays; above it a veil in `background` that fades to the page colour towards the bottom (mockup 19). The play button uses `button`/`onButton`; the liked heart and active toggles (repeat, shuffle, Up Next pill) use `button`; the scrubber and inactive controls stay white/muted as today. Colours fade over about 400 ms on a song change.
- **Download page:** `site/screens/player.html` follows the new look (and `tool/make_screenshots.sh` for the README image), per the CLAUDE.md rule for shown screens.

## Testing

- Unit tests for the colour function: an orange cover gives an orange-hued background and a light orange button; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 for white on `background` and `onButton` on `button`, also for a near-white and a near-black cover; a grey cover gives neutral colours.
- Widget tests for the player sheet with the provider overridden: dark mode with a cover uses `button` for play button and liked heart; light mode, no cover and not-yet-computed colours use the accent.

## Non-goals

Tinting the mini player, lock screen or other screens; storing colours in the database; a setting to turn the tint off.

## Changed after the first build (user, 2026-11-25)

Seen in the first build, the user asked for the Spotify look instead of mockup 19:

- **A flat colour, no gradient:** the player's background is the cover colour (`background`) as one flat colour; the blurred cover and the gradients are gone while it is tinted (they stay for the untinted player).
- **White controls:** play button white with a near-black icon (`tintAccent` / `tintOnAccent`), liked heart and Up Next (on) white. `CoverColors` therefore only carries `background`.
- **Active toggles:** in dark mode an active shuffle/repeat is the accent (white when tinted) with a small dot below instead of a tinted circle (Claude's call: for all of dark mode, so it stays consistent).
- **A dot on the scrubber** at the current position, always (all modes).
