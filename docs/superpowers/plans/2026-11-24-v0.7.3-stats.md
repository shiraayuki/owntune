# v0.7.3 Home: Stats Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the Stats placeholder with real stats built from the `plays` history, following mockups 03, 03b, 04, 04b and 06 and the approved definitions in the spec. The page has:
- period chips
- a listening time card with chart
- streak and songs played cards
- top lists with See All
- time of day
- genres
- the monthly recap
- library overview and most played

**Architecture:** `StatsRepository` returns four bounded, `distinct` SQL aggregates over `plays`, bucketed in local time. Pure builders in `lib/home/stats*.dart` turn them, together with the current `Library`, into a `StatsView`. `statsProvider` keys the streams by day-granular ranges, following the v0.7.2 pattern. `StatsScreen` renders the view.

**Tech Stack:** Flutter 3.50 / Dart 3.14, flutter_riverpod 3, drift `customSelect().watch()` + `.distinct(ListEquality().equals)` (package:collection), flutter_test with `pumpApp`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-v0.7.3-stats-design.md`. It is the authority; read it first, and read its "Orchestrator's call" notes. Mockups are `docs/mockups/home-v1/03*.html`, `04*.html`, `06-stats-empty.html` and `home.css`.

## Global Constraints

**Code and repo rules**
- Imports: `package:material_ui/material_ui.dart` and `package:cupertino_ui/cupertino_ui.dart`.

- `flutter analyze` must report "No issues found!", and `dart format` must leave no changes.
- Dependencies go through providers that tests override (`pumpApp`).
- The injected `clockProvider` is the only "now". There is no `DateTime.now()` in Home code.
- SQL groups in local time with `strftime(…, started_at, 'unixepoch', 'localtime')`. Range filters are `started_at >= ? AND started_at < ?`, using `Variable<DateTime>`.

**What counts**
- A play is a row with `counted = 1`.
- Listening time is `SUM(listened_ms)` over all rows.

**Periods**
- Week starts on Monday 00:00 local.
- The previous range is the same elapsed span one week, month or year earlier. For Month it is capped at the end of the previous month. All Time has no previous range.
- Delta lines are hidden for All Time, and whenever the previous range starts before the first play.

**Exact strings**
- Chips: "Week", "Month", "Year", "All Time".
- Listening card eyebrow: "Listening time · this week|this month|this year|all time".
- Deltas:
  - "+48 min vs. last week" and "−1 h 05 min vs. last month". The minus sign is U+2212 "−".
  - "Same as last year".
  - The Songs played delta: "+12 vs. last week".
- Card labels: "Listening streak", "{n} days", "1 day", "Songs played".
- Average label: "avg {x}".
- Section titles: "Top Artists", "Top Songs", "Top Albums", "See All", "Time of Day", "Genres", "Your Library", "Most Played of All Time".
- Time of Day eyebrow: "You listen most around {8 PM}". Day parts: "Morning", "Afternoon", "Evening", "Night".
- Genres eyebrow: "Genre mix · {n} plays this week|this month|this year|all time".
- Monthly recap:
  - Eyebrow: "Your {November} so far".
  - Caption: "listened in {d} days", then ", {Δ} more than {October}", ", {Δ} less than {October}" or ", same as {October}".
  - Facts: "Top song · {n} plays", then "New finds" with "songs played the first time".
- Your Library card labels: "Songs", "Albums", "Artists", "Playlists", "Total duration", "Storage used".
- Not-enough-history card: "Stats fill up as you listen" / "Play a few songs and your listening time, streak, top artists and favorite hours show up here."
- Error: "Could not load Stats".
- Play counts: "1 play" / "{n} plays".

**Fixed values**
- `formatListening`:
  - "{h} h {mm} min" from 1 h ("6 h 05 min")
  - "{m} min" from 1 min
  - "< 1 min" between 1 ms and 1 min
  - "0 min" at 0
- Day parts: Morning 05–11, Afternoon 12–17, Evening 18–23, Night 00–04. Day-part and genre percentages use largest-remainder rounding and sum to 100.
- Genres: the top 4 plus "Other". Blank genres and deleted songs count as "Unknown".
- List sizes: top lists show 3, See All shows up to 50, Most Played shows 5.
- History threshold: fewer than 5 counted plays in total means not enough history.
- Chart bars:
  - Week: 7 bars, labelled M T W T F S S.
  - Month: one bar per day, labelled 1, 8, 15, 22, 29.
  - Year: 12 bars, labelled J F M A M J J A S O N D.
  - All Time: one bar per month, at most 24, then one bar per year.

**Deleted songs**
- They count in listening time, Songs played, the chart, Time of Day and genres ("Unknown").
- They never appear in the top lists.

## Review Focus

1. **Midnight and DST in local-time bucketing:** a play at 23:59 and one at 00:01 fall on different days. A week that contains the DST change still has 7 bars. → Task 1 test `days are bucketed in local time` and Task 2 test `week range across a DST change`.
2. **Right after the update the history is tiny:**
   - Deltas are hidden, because the previous range predates the history.
   - The not-enough-history state shows below 5 plays.
   - At exactly 5 plays the full page shows.
   → Task 3 test `delta hidden when the previous range predates the history` and Task 5 test `threshold at 5 counted plays`.
3. **A deleted song with plays:**
   - It counts in Songs played and in genres as "Unknown".
   - It is absent from Top Songs, Top Artists and Top Albums.
   - Genre n equals Songs played.
   → Task 4 test `deleted songs count but never rank`.
4. **A renamed artist after plays:** its plays are grouped under the current name, because tags are resolved through the Library. → Task 4 test `renamed artist groups under the current name`.
5. **Playback running while Stats is open:** the 5-second updates of the open row must not rebuild Stats unless an aggregate changed. → Task 1 test `an update outside the range does not re-emit`.

  final class AlbumTarget extends RankTarget { AlbumTarget(this.album); final Album album; }

  class TopLists { final List<RankRow> songs, artists, albums; } // each <= 50, sorted
  TopLists buildTopLists({required Library library, required List<TrackTotal> totals});
  class GenreMix { final String eyebrow; final List<({String name, int percent, int plays})> genres; } // top 4 + 'Other'

  class MonthRecap { final String eyebrow, total, caption; final RankRow? topSong; final int newFinds; }

- Ranking rules (spec "Top Artists / Top Songs / Top Albums"):

  - Artists are grouped by `artistGroupKey(track.artist)` through `library.artist`. The subtitle is the `formatListening` of their summed `listenedMs`, and ties are broken by time.

  - Deleted tracks and tracks without an album are skipped where noted.
