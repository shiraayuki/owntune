# v0.7.3 — Home: Stats (design)

## Intent

This is the last of the three Home releases: v0.7.1 brought Liked Songs and the play history, v0.7.2 brought For You, and **v0.7.3 brings Stats**. The Stats sub-tab replaces its placeholder with a picture of how the user listens: listening time, a daily chart, the streak, top artists, songs and albums for a period, time of day, the genre mix, a monthly recap and a library overview.

Mockups: `docs/mockups/home-v1/03-stats.html`, `03b-stats-rankings.html`, `04-stats-scrolled.html`, `04b-stats-library.html`, `06-stats-empty.html` and the shared `home.css`. The user approved the definitions below in conversation.

The history (`plays`) starts with v0.7.1. "All Time" therefore covers only the time since then for every history-based figure. "Most Played of All Time" is the one exception: it uses the older all-time `tracks.play_count`.

## Goals

- **Period chips** Week | Month | Year | All Time at the top of Stats. They are small chips, not a third tab level. Week is the default. The chips apply to everything on the tab except **Your Library** and **Most Played of All Time**.
- **Listening time card:** total listening time in the period, the change against the previous period, a bar chart, and an average line with a label.
- **Streak card** and **Songs played card** side by side. Songs played shows the change against the previous period.
- **Top Artists, Top Songs, Top Albums:** the top 3 of each, ranked by plays, with a meter bar. Each list has **See All**, which opens the full list (up to 50).
- **Time of Day:** a 24-hour histogram, "You listen most around {hour}", and four day parts in percent.
- **Genres:** the genre mix of the period. The top 4 genres are shown with "Other" for the rest, in percent.
- **Monthly recap** ("Your {Month} so far"): it shows the listening time this month, the days with listening, the comparison with last month, the top song of the month and "New finds".
- **Your Library:** songs, albums, artists, playlists, total duration and storage used.
- **Most Played of All Time:** the top 5 songs by all-time play count.
- **Not enough history:** with fewer than 5 counted plays in total, Stats shows "Stats fill up as you listen" and only Your Library (mockup 06).

## Non-goals

- A yearly "Wrapped" story, sharing or exporting stats, or images of stats.
- Choosing custom date ranges, or browsing past weeks and months (no "previous week" arrows).
- Stats per playlist or per source.
- Backfilling history from before v0.7.1.
- Persisting the selected period across restarts (it lives for the session, like the sub-tab).
- Charting packages: the charts are simple widgets.

## Anti-goals

- Stats that make Home slow or rebuild constantly during playback. The aggregates run in SQL, return bounded results, and emit only when their result changes (the v0.7.2 lesson: `.distinct`).
- Numbers that disagree with each other on screen. For example, the Songs played card must equal the sum the top lists are built from, and the genre "N plays" must equal Songs played for the same period.
- Misleading "New finds" for songs that were played before the history existed.
- A crash or a blank tab when the history fails. History errors show `LoadError('Could not load Stats', …)`, and Your Library keeps working on its own.

## Constraints

- The CLAUDE.md rules apply:
  - logic is testable on Linux without Rust, through providers and `pumpApp`
  - everything is in English
  - imports come from `material_ui` / `cupertino_ui`
  - errors are visible
- **Local time:** days, hours, weeks and months are local time. SQL groups with `strftime(..., started_at, 'unixepoch', 'localtime')`. Period bounds are computed in Dart from the injected `clockProvider` and passed in as `DateTime` variables.
- **Source keys:** `plays.source_key` is not needed for Stats. If it is ever used, the v0.7.1 rule holds: compare it by equality only.
- **What counts:** a "play" is a counted row (`counted = 1`, the 30-second rule). "Listening time" is `SUM(listened_ms)` over all rows, which includes short samples. This matches the approved definitions.

## Approaches

1. **SQL aggregates per period, with grouping by artist/album/genre in Dart against the current `Library`.** *(Chosen)* This is the same split as v0.7.2.
   - A `StatsRepository` returns small aggregated lists: per track, per local day or month, per local hour, and the first counted play per track.
   - Dart maps the track ids to the current tags. That makes renamed artists and albums count under their current name, and deleted tracks drop out of the top lists. Deleted tracks still count toward listening time, because the history keeps them.
   - The results stay bounded: at most one row per played track, per day or per hour.
2. Load the period's rows into Dart and aggregate there. This is simple for a week, but "All Time" and "Year" grow without bound. Rejected (as in v0.7.2).
3. Group by artist, album and genre in SQL by joining `tracks`. The SQL becomes heavier, and the grouping keys (`artistGroupKey`, `albumKeyOf`, display names) would be duplicated in SQL. Rejected.

## Design

### Periods

`enum StatsPeriod { week, month, year, allTime }`. Each period has a current range `[from, to)` and a previous range used for comparisons. `now` comes from `clockProvider`.

- **Week:** the current range runs from Monday 00:00 of this week to `now`. The previous range is the same elapsed span one week earlier: from last Monday 00:00 to `now − 7 days`. *(Orchestrator's call: comparing a partial week with a full one would always look like less.)*
- **Month:** the current range runs from the 1st of this month at 00:00 to `now`. The previous range covers the same number of elapsed days in the previous month, and is capped at the end of that month.
- **Year:** the current range runs from January 1 at 00:00 to `now`. The previous range is the same elapsed span one year earlier.
- **All Time:** the current range runs from the first `plays.started_at` to `now`. There is no previous range and no delta line.

The chips show the labels "Week", "Month", "Year" and "All Time".

### Data (`lib/home/stats_repository.dart`, `statsRepositoryProvider`)

All four streams are drift `customSelect(...).watch()` with `readsFrom: {plays}`, and each is followed by `.distinct(ListEquality().equals)`.

- `watchTrackTotals({required DateTime from, required DateTime to})`
  - Returns `List<TrackTotal(trackId, plays, listenedMs)>`, where `plays` = counted rows and `listenedMs` = the sum over all rows in the range.
  - It feeds the top lists, Songs played, the genres and the top song of the month.
- `watchListeningBuckets({required DateTime from, required DateTime to, required StatsBucket bucket})`
  - Returns `List<ListeningBucket(DateTime start, int listenedMs, int plays)>`, one per local day (`'%Y-%m-%d'`) or local month (`'%Y-%m'`) that has rows.
  - It feeds the chart and the listening total.
- `watchHourly({required DateTime from, required DateTime to})`
  - Returns `List<int>` of length 24 with the `listenedMs` per local hour.
- `watchTrackHistory()` *(orchestrator's call, replaces a first-plays-only query)*
  - Returns `Map<int, ({DateTime first, int counted})>`: per track over all time, the first counted play (`MIN(started_at)` over counted rows) and the number of counted rows. Tracks without counted rows are absent.
  - It feeds New finds. It is bounded by one row per played track.
  - The streak needs all days that have counted plays. `watchListeningBuckets` over All Time by day with `countedOnly: true` (only `counted = 1` rows) provides those.
- `watchRowsOfDay({required DateTime day})`
  - Returns `List<({DateTime startedAt, bool counted, int listenedMs})>`: every row of the local day `[day, next midnight)`.
  - It feeds the part of the previous range's last day before the exact cut (see `statsProvider`).
- `watchCountedPlays()`
  - Returns the number of counted rows in the whole history (`SELECT COUNT(*) … WHERE counted = 1`), for the not-enough-history threshold.

Range filters use `started_at >= ? AND started_at < ?`, so the `plays_started` index applies.

A pure function in `lib/home/stats.dart` builds the view model:

```
StatsView buildStats({
  required StatsPeriod period, required DateTime now,
  required Library library, required int playlistCount, required int storageBytes,
  required List<TrackTotal> current, required List<TrackTotal>? previous,
  required List<ListeningBucket> buckets, required List<ListeningBucket>? previousBuckets,
  required List<ListeningBucket> allDays,          // All Time by day, for the streak
  required List<int> hourly,
  required List<TrackTotal> month, required List<TrackTotal> previousMonth,
  required Map<int, ({DateTime first, int counted})> trackHistory, required int totalCountedPlays,
  required DateTime? firstPlayAt,                  // earliest plays.started_at, null without history
})
```

`statsProvider` (`Provider<AsyncValue<StatsView>>`) gets the period from `statsPeriodProvider`, which holds the chip selection for the session. It keys every stream provider by day-granular bounds, following the v0.7.2 pattern: a `.family` keyed by the range start and today's date. That keeps the streams stable within a day. The provider stays loading until every history stream has a value.

The previous range ends exactly at `previousRange(period, now).to`, not at the end of that day. Its whole days come from the day-keyed streams up to the comparison day's midnight; that day's rows come from `watchRowsOfDay` (keyed by the day), and only the rows that started before the cut are added in Dart to the previous listening time and Songs played.

Home's sub-tabs stay mounted, but a hidden one (the other sub-tab, or Home not being the selected tab) runs with `TickerMode` off, from `Visibility.of`. Riverpod 3 pauses what a consumer watches while its ticker mode is off, so the hidden page's queries stop fetching during playback and catch up when it shows again.

### Sections

**Listening time card**
- Eyebrow:
  - Week: "Listening time · this week"
  - Month: "Listening time · this month"
  - Year: "Listening time · this year"
  - All Time: "Listening time · all time"
- The big number uses `formatListening`:
  - "6 h 12 min" when there is at least one hour
  - "54 min" for at least one minute
  - "< 1 min" for more than 0 but under a minute
  - "0 min" for nothing
- Delta line. It is hidden for All Time, and hidden when the previous range starts before `firstPlayAt`, because the history does not fully cover it. *(Orchestrator's call: right after the update, "+6 h vs. last month" against a month without history would mislead.)* When the previous range is covered and both values are less than a minute apart (also both 0), it reads "Same as …", never "+< 1 min".
  - "+48 min vs. last week" / "−1 h 05 min vs. last month" / "Same as last year"
  - It compares the current range with the previous range.
- Chart:
  - Week: 7 bars Mon–Sun, labelled M T W T F S S
  - Month: one bar per day of the month, labelled 1, 8, 15, 22, 29
  - Year: 12 bars, labelled J F M A M J J A S O N D
  - All Time: one bar per month from the first play to now, at most 24. If there are more, it uses one bar per year. Labels show the month (or year) of the first and last bar.
  - Future days and months show as empty slots.
  - Bars are scaled to the maximum.
- Average:
  - The line sits at the mean over elapsed bars that have data.
  - The label reads "avg 53 min" (per day for Week and Month) or "avg 12 h" (per month for Year and All Time).

**Streak card**
- The streak counts consecutive local days with at least one counted play, ending today. If there is nothing today yet, it ends yesterday.
- Text: "{n} days" / "1 day" / "0 days", with the label "Listening streak".
- The dots show the last 7 days, filled where a day had a counted play.
- The streak ignores the period chips.

**Songs played card**
- The count of counted plays in the period, with the label "Songs played".
- Delta: "+12 vs. last week" / "−3 vs. last month" / "Same as last year". The same hiding rule applies as for the listening time delta.

**Deleted songs** *(orchestrator's call)*
- Their history stays, so they still count in listening time, in Songs played, in the chart, in Time of Day and in the genre mix, where they go under "Unknown".
- They cannot appear in the top lists, because they have no tags to show.
- So the genre "N plays" equals Songs played, and the top lists are a subset.

**Top Artists / Top Songs / Top Albums** (top 3 each)
- **Songs**
  - A `TrackTotal` resolved to a track; deleted tracks are skipped.
  - Sorted by plays descending, then `listenedMs` descending.
  - Subtitle: the artist. Right side: "{n} plays" / "1 play".
- **Artists**
  - Grouped by `artistGroupKey(track.artist)` through `library.artist(key)` (the same grouping as the Artists tab).
  - The sum of plays and `listenedMs`, sorted by plays, then time.
  - Subtitle: the listening time. Right side: "{n} plays".
  - Artwork: the artist avatar, round.
- **Albums**
  - Grouped by `albumKeyOf(track)`; songs without an album tag are skipped.
  - The sum of plays, sorted by plays.
  - Subtitle: the album artist. Right side: "{n} plays". Artwork: the cover.
- Each list's meter bar is proportional to its #1.
- **See All** opens a list page with the same rows, up to 50, with the `NavRow` back label "Home". Tapping a row:
  - a song plays the full list from it, with `QueueSource(QueueSourceKind.other, 'Top Songs')`
  - an artist or album opens its page, with the list title ("Top Artists", "Top Albums") as back label; from the Stats page itself the back label is "Home"
- While Stats loads again (a new day, a new period), the list page keeps its last rows.
- A list with no items is hidden.

**Time of Day**
- A 24-bar histogram of `listenedMs` per local hour, with axis labels 12 AM, 6 AM, 12 PM, 6 PM, 12 AM.
- Eyebrow: "You listen most around {h}", where h is the peak hour as "8 PM" / "12 AM".
- Four parts in percent of listening time, rounded with largest-remainder so they sum to 100:
  - Morning 05–11
  - Afternoon 12–17
  - Evening 18–23
  - Night 00–04
- The largest part is highlighted, as `.hi` is in the mockup.
- Hidden when the period has no listening.

**Genres**
- Counted plays per genre tag. The tag is trimmed and grouped case-insensitively under its most common spelling. Blank tags and deleted songs become "Unknown".
- The top 4 are shown, then "Other" with the rest when anything remains. All are in percent of the period's counted plays, rounded with largest-remainder.
- Eyebrow: "Genre mix · {n} plays this week|this month|this year|all time" ("1 play" for one), where n equals Songs played.
- A stacked bar (`.mix`) plus the list.
- Hidden without plays.

**Monthly recap** (always the current calendar month, independent of the chips)
- Eyebrow: "Your {November} so far".
- Big number: this month's listening time.
- Caption: "listened in {d} days" (days with any listening), then ", {Δ} more than {October}" / ", {Δ} less than {October}" / ", same as {October}".
  - The comparison uses the full previous month. Less than a minute apart reads ", same as {October}".
  - It is left out when the previous month has no history.
- Fact 1: the top song of the month, with "Top song · {n} plays".
- Fact 2: "New finds" with "songs played the first time". The count is the tracks in the library whose `trackHistory.first` falls in this month **and** whose `play_count` equals `trackHistory.counted`, so they had no plays from before the history existed. *(Orchestrator's call: this excludes songs played before v0.7.1.)*
- The recap is hidden while this month has no listening.

**Your Library** (always shown)
- A grid of six cards:
  - Songs (`library.tracks.length`)
  - Albums
  - Artists
  - Playlists (count of playlists)
  - Total duration (`formatListening` of the sum of `durationMs`)
  - Storage used (`formatBytes` from `lib/settings/settings_sheet.dart`)
- The storage value comes from `TrackRepository.storageBytes()` through a `FutureProvider` that is re-run when the set of files changes (a hash of every track's `fileName` and `coverFile`), not on a play count. While it loads, the card shows "…".

**Most Played of All Time**
- The top 5 tracks by `playCount` (> 0), ties broken by `lastPlayedAt`. Subtitle: the artist. Right side: "{n} plays".
- There is no See All. Tapping a row plays these 5, with `QueueSource(other, 'Most Played')`.
- Hidden when nothing was played.

**Not enough history** (`totalCountedPlays < 5`)
- Only the mockup-06 card is shown: "Stats fill up as you listen" / "Play a few songs and your listening time, streak, top artists and favorite hours show up here."
- Your Library follows below it.
- The chips are hidden.

**Empty library**
- Stats shows the same empty state as For You (Import Music / Search Online).

### Errors

- A history stream error shows `LoadError('Could not load Stats', …)` in place of the history sections.
- Your Library still renders from the library.

### Testing

**Unit: `buildStats`**
- period ranges and previous ranges for each period, including a month whose previous month is shorter
- Week starting on Monday
- delta texts
- `formatListening` boundaries
- chart buckets, including future slots and the All Time switch to years after 24 months
- average over elapsed bars
- streak: today empty vs. yesterday, and a gap
- the top lists' grouping and order, with deleted tracks skipped and songs without an album skipped
- Time of Day peak hour and parts summing to 100
- genres:
  - case folding
  - "Unknown"
  - "Other"
  - sum = 100
  - n = Songs played
- New finds excludes songs with plays from before the history
- recap caption variants
- the < 5 plays threshold

**Unit: `StatsRepository`** (in-memory database)
- every query, including local-time bucketing
- range bounds: inclusive `from`, exclusive `to`
- `distinct`: an update that only changes `listened_ms` of a row outside the range causes no re-emit

**Widget**
- the chips switch the period and the texts follow
- See All opens the full list, and a row tap plays or opens
- the not-enough-history state
- an error shows "Could not load Stats" while Your Library still shows
- empty sections are hidden

**Integration**
- Stats on the real database with inserted plays
